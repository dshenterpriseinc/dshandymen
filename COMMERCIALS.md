# DS Handymen, Inc. — 20 Mascot Commercials
### Style-test slate. The Bear (and the Bird) star in every one. No humans, ever.

**Purpose:** produce 20 short spots across deliberately different visual styles so Dave can
see the range and pick a direction before we commit to a house style for the website and
social.

**Hard rules baked into every prompt**
- The **polar bear mascot is the only character** (the Pigeon appears in the design-side spots)
- **No humans, no faces, no crowds** — anywhere, including backgrounds
- **No on-screen text, no logos, no watermarks** — added later in edit, so clips stay reusable
- Consistent character: friendly, muscular **white polar bear** in a **deep navy work shirt**
- Palette: navy `#1B2A4A` · royal blue `#00338D` · charcoal `#333333` · white ·
  red `#C60C30` · safety yellow `#F5B324` **on equipment only**
- Setting: Western New York / Southtowns — modest two-storey homes, lake-effect snow

**Character block reused in every prompt:**
> A friendly muscular cartoon POLAR BEAR mascot wearing a deep navy work shirt — white fur,
> black nose, warm confident expression. He is the only character in the scene.

## FULL PROMPTS

**01 · LAKE EFFECT** — *photoreal cinematic, plowing*
Pre-dawn blue hour, suburban Western New York street buried in fresh lake-effect snow. A
friendly muscular cartoon polar bear in a deep navy work shirt drives a navy pickup truck
fitted with a safety-yellow plow blade, pushing a clean wall of powder toward camera.
Headlights cut through drifting snow, powder sprays off the blade catching the light. The
bear is photorealistically composited into a live-action-quality scene — believable lighting,
real snow physics, shallow depth of field, slow forward dolly, film grain. Modest two-storey
homes with snow-loaded roofs behind. No humans, no text, no logos.

**02 · THE BEAR WAKES UP** — *Pixar 3D, plowing*
Pixar-quality 3D animation. Interior of a cosy dark bedroom at night. A friendly muscular
polar bear is fast asleep. Wind howls; snow batters the window. One eye snaps open. Smash
cut: the bear, now in a deep navy work shirt, grabs a set of truck keys off a hook and
strides out the door with purpose. Warm interior light against cold blue exterior, expressive
character animation, cinematic depth of field. The bear is the only character. No text.

**03 · DON'T GET STUCK** — *2D flat cartoon, plowing*
Bold flat 2D vector animation, thick confident outlines, limited palette of navy, royal blue,
white and safety yellow. A small car's wheels spin uselessly in a deep snowdrift, snow
spraying. A friendly polar bear in a navy work shirt arrives in a plow truck and sweeps the
drift away in one clean pass, leaving a perfect clear driveway. He turns to camera and gives
a confident wink and thumbs up. Snappy, punchy cartoon timing. No humans, no text.

**04 · SATISFYING REVEAL** — *photoreal macro, pressure washing*
Photorealistic macro slow-motion. A friendly muscular cartoon polar bear in a deep navy work
shirt grips a pressure washer wand and sweeps it across filthy grey weathered concrete,
revealing a bright clean stripe in one satisfying pass. Water atomising into mist, grime
lifting, droplets frozen in warm side sunlight. The bear is realistically lit and composited
into the real-world scene. Extremely satisfying texture reveal, shallow depth of field.
No humans, no text, no logos.

**05 · HOUSE TRANSFORMATION** — *Pixar 3D, pressure washing*
Pixar-quality 3D animation. A dingy, algae-streaked two-storey suburban house looks tired
and grey. A friendly polar bear in a deep navy work shirt raises a pressure washer; a sweeping
arc of water passes across the frame like a wipe transition, and everything it touches turns
brilliantly clean and bright. The bear plants his paws proudly. Warm sunlight breaks through.
Expressive, joyful character animation. The bear is the only character. No text.

**06 · TOOLBELT SHUFFLE** — *retro 1950s rubber-hose cartoon, handyman*
Vintage 1950s rubber-hose cartoon animation, slightly grainy film texture, muted retro
palette with navy and warm cream. A cheerful polar bear in a navy work shirt and tool belt
bounces rhythmically as he hammers a nail, spins a screwdriver, and drills a shelf — every
motion perfectly on the beat, bouncy squash-and-stretch, whistling body language. Old-cartoon
charm, hand-inked look. The bear is the only character. No text, no logos.

**07 · THREE-SEASON ROOM** — *photoreal cinematic, sunrooms*
Photorealistic cinematic. Golden hour in a leafy Western New York backyard. A friendly
muscular cartoon polar bear in a deep navy work shirt grips a large canvas tarp and pulls it
away in one smooth motion, revealing a beautiful modern glass-walled three-season sunroom
with a retractable glass patio enclosure. Warm low sunlight floods through the glass, autumn
trees behind. The bear is photorealistically lit and composited. Slow cinematic reveal,
shallow depth of field. No humans, no text.

**08 · THE BIRD ARRIVES** — *Pixar 3D, Pigeon Division*
Pixar-quality 3D animation. A plain, unfinished, drab interior room. An elegant grey-and-tan
cartoon PIGEON character wearing a small navy work vest flies in and lands, carrying a rolled
blueprint and a paintbrush. She unrolls the blueprint; where her paintbrush sweeps, the room
transforms — crisp white trim, custom cabinetry, warm tile, finished paint. She tilts her head
proudly. Warm designer lighting, elegant and refined rather than rough. The pigeon is the only
character. No humans, no text.

**09 · BEAR & BIRD** — *2D animated split-screen, whole company*
Bold flat 2D vector animation, thick outlines, navy and charcoal palette. Split screen: on the
LEFT a muscular polar bear in a navy work shirt swings a sledgehammer and hauls lumber,
powerful and rugged. On the RIGHT an elegant pigeon in a navy vest lays tile, brushes on paint
and fits fine trim, precise and graceful. The dividing line slides away and the two halves
merge into one beautifully finished room with both characters standing together. Muscle meets
craft. No humans, no text.

**10 · ATTIC RESCUE** — *claymation stop-motion, house clearance*
Charming handmade claymation stop-motion with visible fingerprint texture and slight frame
jitter. A cluttered attic crammed with boxes, old furniture and junk. A friendly clay polar
bear in a navy work shirt cheerfully carries armload after armload out in fast stop-motion,
boxes vanishing one by one, until the attic is completely clean, empty and full of light. He
dusts his paws together, satisfied. Warm tactile handmade look. The bear is the only character.
No text.

**11 · LEAF FALL** — *tilt-shift miniature, landscaping*
Tilt-shift miniature diorama look — extremely shallow focus, high saturation, everything
appears like a tiny handcrafted model. A miniature autumn Western New York yard with a small
house, brilliant orange and red trees. A tiny polar bear in a navy work shirt rakes swirling
leaves into neat piles as more leaves drift down. Charming, cosy, model-village aesthetic,
slow gentle camera drift. The bear is the only character. No humans, no text.

**12 · GUTTER PATROL** — *comic book cel-shaded, gutter cleanout*
Bold comic-book cel-shaded animation with heavy black ink outlines, halftone dot shading and
dramatic low-angle hero framing. A muscular polar bear in a navy work shirt climbs a ladder

downspout. Dynamic superhero-style angles, dramatic sky behind, action-comic energy. The bear
is the only character. No text, no speech bubbles.

**13 · GO BILLS** — *bold 2D cartoon, plowing / brand*
Bold flat 2D cartoon animation in Buffalo Bills royal blue, red and white. Game day in a snowy
Western New York neighbourhood. A polar bear in a navy work shirt with a red-white-and-blue
scarf plows a driveway, and the cleared shape he leaves behind resembles a football field with
goalposts. He raises both paws in triumph, snow confetti bursting around him. Energetic,
celebratory, big-game energy. The bear is the only character. No humans, no text.

**14 · PERFECT STRIPES** — *anime, mowing*
Dramatic Japanese anime animation style, cel-shaded with bold speed lines, dynamic camera
angles and lens flares. A determined polar bear in a navy work shirt pushes a lawn mower
across an overgrown lawn with intense focus, grass clippings flying in dramatic arcs. Wide
reveal: an immaculate lawn with perfect alternating mown stripes. Epic, over-the-top,
sports-anime intensity applied to lawn care. The bear is the only character. No text.

**15 · KITCHEN GLOW-UP** — *photoreal + mascot, remodeling*
Photorealistic cinematic interior. A dated, tired kitchen. A friendly muscular cartoon polar
bear in a deep navy work shirt lifts a new shaker cabinet door into place and fits it
perfectly. Smooth push-in reveal to a beautifully finished modern kitchen — new cabinets,
counters, warm pendant lighting. The bear is photorealistically lit and composited into the
real interior, stands back and nods approvingly. Warm, aspirational, real-estate-quality
cinematography. No humans, no text.

**16 · DECK REBORN** — *Pixar 3D, deck refinishing*
Pixar-quality 3D animation. A grey, splintered, weathered backyard deck. A friendly polar bear
in a navy work shirt power-sands it, then draws a brush of rich warm stain across the boards —
colour blooming outward across the whole deck as he goes. Finished, he flops into a deck chair
with a tall glass of lemonade, deeply content. Golden late-afternoon light, warm and satisfying.
The bear is the only character. No humans, no text.

**17 · WINDOW SWAP** — *stop-motion papercraft, windows*
Handmade papercraft stop-motion animation — everything built from layered coloured paper and
card with visible cut edges and paper texture. A paper house on a cold blue snowy night, with

lifts out the old window and slots in a crisp new one. The interior instantly glows warm golden

**18 · THE SNOW RULER** — *flat motion graphic, plowing / seasonal*
Clean flat vector motion-graphic animation, navy, white and safety yellow, minimal and modern.
A friendly cartoon polar bear in a navy work shirt stands beside a giant measuring ruler stuck
in the ground as snow steadily piles higher and higher up the markings — 6 inches, 12 inches,

No humans, no on-screen numbers or text.

**19 · 3 A.M. CALL** — *photoreal noir, storm response*
Photorealistic cinematic night scene, moody and high-contrast. A heavy snowstorm at 3 a.m. in
a dark, silent Western New York neighbourhood. A parked navy pickup truck with a plow sits in
darkness. Suddenly the headlights snap on, cutting two hard beams through the falling snow. A
friendly muscular cartoon polar bear in a deep navy work shirt is at the wheel, calm and
determined. The plow blade drops with authority and the truck pulls forward into the storm.
Dramatic noir lighting, cold blues against warm headlight glow. No humans, no text.

**20 · THE BADGE** — *3D logo sting, brand end card*
Premium 3D logo animation on a deep charcoal background. Machined metal pieces fly in and
assemble into a circular badge emblem with a clean outer ring. A stylised muscular polar bear
in a navy work shirt steps into the centre of the badge and folds his arms confidently,
settling into a strong heroic pose as a subtle light sweep passes across the metal. Premium,
weighty, broadcast-quality brand sting. Charcoal, navy and white only. Leave the ring area
clear for text to be added later. The bear is the only character. No text, no lettering.

## THE END CARD — house standard

**Decision: one common end card, style-matched body.** Real local-TV campaigns keep the
branded slate identical across every spot — the phone number lands in the same place every
time, which is what actually builds recall. The *body* of each spot carries its own style;
the last 5 seconds are always the same.

> ⚠️ **Never let the video model render the text.** Veo garbles letterforms — it will produce
> `716-B03-009I` or `dshandymun.com`. Every end card is composited in ffmpeg with real
> typography so the phone number and URL are always pixel-perfect and correct.

**Structure — 5.0s, cut in with a 0.4s dissolve (total spot 14.6s ≈ a broadcast :15)**

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Background | Deep navy `#16253F`, flat |
| Badge | White knockout bear badge, 250px, screen-blended, centred at top |
| Tagline | Safety yellow `#F5B324`, Arial Bold 38px — **changes per spot** |
| Website | `dshandymen.com` — white, Segoe UI Black 64px |
| Phone | `(716) 803-0091` — white, Segoe UI Black 64px |
| Towns | `HAMBURG · BLASDELL · ORCHARD PARK · LACKAWANNA` — 24px, pale blue |
| Trust | `FULLY INSURED · BBB A+ RATED · SERVING WNY SINCE 2009` — 21px |

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- Each generated clip is **~8 seconds**. Cut to **:10 / :15 / :30** in edit by combining
  spots and adding the **#20 badge sting** as a shared end card.
- Clips ship **silent and text-free on purpose** — voiceover, the existing Buffalo radio
  ad, captions, phone number and CTA get added in edit, so one clip serves TV, web hero,
  Facebook Reel and YouTube pre-roll.
- Suggested **:30 build** — `#19 3 A.M. Call` (tease) → `#01 Lake Effect` (proof) →
  `#03 Don't Get Stuck` (benefit) → `#20 The Badge` (CTA).
- Suggested **social cut-downs** — `#04` and `#16` are the most "oddly satisfying" and will
  travel furthest on Reels.
- Style verdict to make after review: **photoreal** builds trust, **Pixar** builds warmth,
  **flat 2D** is cheapest to iterate and most on-brand with his existing infographics.
