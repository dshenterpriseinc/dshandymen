About

50 years in Hamburg. 16 years in business.

Dave Schultz — "the Bear" — has lived in Hamburg his whole life. Since November 1, 2009, DS Handymen, Inc. has been the number Southtowns homeowners call when something needs plowing, washing, fixing or building.

The Bear

Ask around Hamburg and somebody knows Dave. He's the guy out plowing at 4am when the lake dumps overnight, the truck you see washing a driveway on South Park in April, the one who actually calls you back.

DS Handymen, Inc. is BBB Accredited with an A+ rating (since 2021), fully insured, and holds a 4.7★ rating from 13 reviews. Payment is easy: cash, cheque or Venmo.
